


The Evolution of Decision Intelligence: From Segmentation to Next Best Action to Agentic AI
Stage 1 — Segmentation
Which customers look alike?
Stage 2 — Rules-Based Targeting
Who is eligible for which treatment?
Stage 3 — Predictive Decisioning
Who is most likely to respond, churn, upgrade, or engage?
Stage 4 — Next Best Action
Among all eligible actions, which is most appropriate for this customer?
Stage 5 — Adaptive / Real-Time Decisioning
What is best given what is happening now?
Stage 6 — Agentic Orchestration
What workflow should be pursued, which tools should be invoked, and what should happen next?
The important distinction I'd preserve throughout is:
NBA selects among actions.
Agentic systems may determine the workflow itself.
That's a very useful conceptual distinction.
You could show the architectural evolution as:
Segments → Rules → Models → Decision Engine → Context-Aware NBA → Agentic Orchestration
And underneath it:
Batch → near-real-time → real-time → autonomous/semi-autonomous
Agentic AI does not necessarily replace NBA.
A strong architecture is often:
Agentic orchestration → specialized decision engine → action execution
For example, an AI agent may decide that the interaction requires a retention workflow, while a specialized NBA engine determines the appropriate retention treatment.
That is a nuanced point and would make the article stronger than the usual agents replace everything story.
